# Dino Realms concept generation

Tool: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`, not CLI/API fallback. Concepts are saved in `public/realms/` and integrated into the local prototype. Original outputs remain in the tool's generated-images directory.

The ten replacement habitat landscapes are saved individually in `public/realms/worlds/`, all 1536×1024. Their complete prompts and reference asset are recorded in [realms-world-prompts.json](realms-world-prompts.json). Each was generated separately using the main island as the scale/style reference. They replace the small atlas below in both gameplay and the world selector; the atlas is retained only as historical concept art.
